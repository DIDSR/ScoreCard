Constraint Violation Assessment

This analysis measures how often synthetic feature values violate bounds derived from the real dataset. For each feature, lower and upper limits are set from the 1st and 99th percentiles of real data; synthetic values outside that range count as violations.

  • Constraint Violation Rate — The percentage of synthetic samples that fall below the real-data lower bound or above the upper bound.
  • Lower / Upper Bound — Percentile-based limits estimated from the real feature distribution.

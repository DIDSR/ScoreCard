Congruence

Real vs Synthetic Statistical Congruence

This report presents quantitative measures and visualizations of how congruent (statistically similar) the synthetic dataset is to the real dataset. It typically includes metrics and plots that summarize agreement in feature relationships, moments, or divergence measures between the two distributions.

  • Jensen-Shannon Divergence (JSD) — A symmetric, bounded measure of the difference between the probability distributions of real and synthetic features; values closer to 0 indicate that the two distributions are more similar.
  • Earth Mover's Distance (EMD) / Wasserstein Distance — Quantifies the minimum "work" required to transform the synthetic feature distribution into the real feature distribution; lower values indicate that the two distributions are more closely aligned, with less distributional shift between real and synthetic data.
  • Cosine Similarity — Measures the angular similarity between the real and synthetic feature vectors; values closer to 1 indicate that the two datasets point in the same direction in feature space, implying strong directional agreement.

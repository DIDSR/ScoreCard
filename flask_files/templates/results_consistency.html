Consistency

Cross-Group Consistency Assessment

This analysis measures how consistent feature distributions are across different subgroups. Lower variance and smaller max-min differences indicate more consistent synthetic data quality across groups.

  • Variance of Group Means — Measures how much the per-subgroup means vary from one another (computed with ddof=1); lower variance indicates that the metric behaves similarly across all subgroups, suggesting more equitable representation.
  • Max-Min Difference — The range between the highest and lowest subgroup means; a smaller difference indicates that no single subgroup is dramatically over- or under-represented relative to others.
  • ANOVA F-Statistic — The test statistic from a one-way ANOVA across all subgroups; a higher F-statistic suggests that between-group differences are large relative to within-group variation, indicating inconsistency across subgroups.

Synthetic Data Coverage Assessment

This analysis visualizes the extent to which the synthetic dataset covers the feature space of the real data. It helps identify regions of high and low support, highlighting potential gaps or over-representation in the generated synthetic samples.

  • Variance — Measures the overall spread of normalized feature values across all samples; higher variance suggests the dataset occupies a broader range of the feature space.
  • Entropy — Computed on the mean feature vector (normalized to sum to 1), this quantifies the uniformity of feature importance across dimensions; higher entropy indicates more evenly distributed feature contributions.
  • Distance to Centroid — The average pairwise distance from each sample to the mean (centroid) of the normalized feature array; larger values indicate that samples are more spread out from the center of the distribution.
  • Convex Hull Volume — Computed on the first two PCA components of the normalized data, estimating the geometric extent of the dataset in 2D reduced space; a larger volume suggests broader coverage of the feature space.
